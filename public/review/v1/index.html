Questions owners ask us

Something else on your mind? Bring it to the free consultation.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set the system up, run it and explain what it does in plain English. You tell us how you want customers handled.

Will it work with the tools I already use?

We start from your existing systems, such as your phone, website forms, email and calendar, and build on them when they do the job. We only suggest something new when what you have is part of the leak.

Does the AI talk to my customers without me?

Only in the ways you agree to. You decide which questions it can answer, when it should book, and which conversations come straight to you.

How much does it cost?

It is a monthly service, and the price depends on the system you need. We will talk it through in the free consultation, once we understand where your inquiries are being lost.

Do you only build websites?

No. We start with the money leak. If the leak is your website, we build a better one. If it is slow replies or missed follow-up, we fix that first.

What happens in the free consultation?

It is a 30-minute video call on Google Meet. We ask how customers reach you today, where inquiries go and how they turn into bookings. Then we tell you where we see a leak and whether we can help fix it.
